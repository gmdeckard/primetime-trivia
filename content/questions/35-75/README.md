# Questions for 35 to 75

The adult bank, and the bulk of the whole thing. This was two folders — a
55-75 set and a 35-55 set written to be deliberately separate from it — and
they were merged because the separation did not survive contact with the
arithmetic: somebody who is sixty-five today was twenty-five in 1991. Live
Aid, MTV, the Berlin Wall coming down and Nirvana are their adult life, not
their children's.

So one adult group covers the room. The upper end has lived through
everything the lower end has.

What that means for writing:

- Formative years for this group span roughly 1961 to 2010. Weight toward
  that window; it is wide on purpose.
- The old 55-75 rule of "nothing after about 1995" is retired. A 2005
  hurricane or a 2001 scandal is fair game.
- What still does not belong is the narrow end of any era — a particular
  skateboard trick, one synthesizer model, a chart rivalry between two bands.
  Those are the questions that leave two thirds of a room watching. The test
  is not "when did this happen" but "would most of the room have noticed it at
  the time".

Questions carrying an `a35-` id prefix came from the old 35-55 set. The prefix
no longer names a category or a group and is kept only so the ids stay stable;
everything else is prefixed by its category.
