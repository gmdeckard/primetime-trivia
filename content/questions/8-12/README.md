# Questions for 8-12

Written for children of 8 to 12.

The difficulty numbers are relative to that age, not to the adult bank: a 4
here is a hard question for a ten-year-old, not a hard question full stop.

Ground rules kept throughout: nothing that needs adult life experience,
nothing frightening, and no alcohol, politics or war casualties. Every fact
is written to be read aloud and to be the bit a child repeats afterwards.

Eight categories, ten each, shaped 2/5/3 so any one fills a round alone.

## Packs

The questions sit in `kids1/`, a pack like the adult sets. It used to be the
one part of the bank carrying no pack at all, which made it the one part the
MC could not switch off — it was held out of an adult night only by the age
group, and the setup screen showed no switch for it. Now every
multiple-choice question belongs to a pack and the screen shows the lot.
